Seen & Heard

"Not every story is loud, but every story matters." This space is dedicated to the quiet fighters, the hidden talents, the dreamers who’ve been told to dim their light. We see you. We hear you. Seen & Heard is where young people finally get the spotlight — not for who the world expects them to be, but for who they truly are. Whether you're an artist forced to follow someone else's path, a student battling silently with ambition but no support, or a young voice just wanting to be heard — this is your space. Because here, we believe that being unseen doesn't mean you're unworthy. 💬 Watch. Listen. Share. Be Moved.



“I created this space because I once felt unseen too. Let’s rise together.” – Zanele Sbongile Radebe

Siziwe

Hi My name is Siziwe Tshabalala,I am 20 years old. Born on the 6th of April

People often describe me as a good person and say I am quite quiet, but I usually see myself a bit differently. I like to think I'm actually kind, generous, funny, and very talkative! The funny truth is that I sometimes struggle to speak quite as smoothly as I'd like, which is probably why people assume I'm reserved, when in reality, I have a lot to say once I'm comfortable. In my free time, you will most likely find me completely absorbed in solving a challenging sudoku puzzle or baking something sweet in the kitchen. Beyond that, I have a deep curiosity for discovering new facts and picking up new skills whenever I get the chance. Oh, and just one little fun fact to top it all off: I am a ballerina!

Boitumelo

Hey everyone! I'm Boitumelo Tsekelo, and I'm 21 years old. I'm currently figuring things out and working hard every day, but that's only one piece of the puzzle for me.

If you ask the people who know me best, they'd probably tell you I'm pretty passionate, kind, caring, and always trying to be considerate of others. Outside of work, it's the simple things that keep me grounded. I absolutely love singing, there's just something about music that heals the soul and I'm definitely a big fan of good food! Most importantly, my faith is everything to me. You'll often find me at church or pouring my energy into things that draw me closer to God, while also putting in the work to build a future and career I can be proud of. Just taking it one day at a time!

Thato

Hi, My Name is Thato Sphamandla Guda that's my last name. I am 21 years older.

I am very much of a good person I can say, humble, down to earth, I listen to myself a lot, An extrovert person who likes to explore personalities, how the world is and how people are to people. I started my lower Grades at Izibuko Primary School,enrolled for my higher grades in Mpontsheng Secondary School that's where I completed my Matric furthermore went to tertiary level where I completed my mechanical Engineering Diploma. As a young Adult I am still on my twenties trying by all means to fulfill my goals and dreams, it's just perseverance and hope that got me to where I am today. With all my heart I thank God for giving me this opportunity and myself for not giving up since day one.
